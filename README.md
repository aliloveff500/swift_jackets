# SWIFT JACKETS — Luxury Outerwear 3D Showcase

A modern, high-performance luxury e-commerce landing page for **Swift Jackets**, engineered with dynamic ambient lighting, 3D interactive rotation, fluid direction-aware slide animations, and an integrated in-cart checkout flow.

## ✨ Features
- **Dynamic Color Morphing:** Ambient background lighting and gradients morph seamlessly across 4 colorways:
  - Solar Ember (Amber Orange) - $149
  - Stealth Obsidian (Charcoal Black) - $189
  - Glacier Pearl (Silver White) - $169
  - Crimson Ruby (Velvet Red) - $159
- **3D Interactive Showcase:** Click and drag the hero jacket to rotate with realistic depth and shadow dynamics.
- **Cinematic Transitions:** Bottom-right thumbnail physically expands and animates into the hero stage.
- **Flanking Navigation:** Left and right navigation arrows centered on both sides of the jacket.
- **Cart & Checkout Drawer:** Instant add-to-cart flight animation, order placement form, and live in-cart order tracking timeline.
- **All Products Catalog:** Modal showcasing the full outerwear collection with 1-click 3D hero inspection.
